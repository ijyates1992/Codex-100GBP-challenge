# Frozen continuous £500 test protocol

This is one continuous-account Strategy Tester run from **2019.01.01 inclusive** to **2026.09.14 exclusive**. The end date includes the last completed Friday, 2026.09.11, and the intervening weekend; it does not include intraday Monday data.

The purpose is to measure the frozen strategy at a £500 initial balance. The only changed Tester setting is `Deposit=500`; the Expert Advisor source, compiled binary, inputs, 100 ms execution delay, Model 4 real-tick setting, currency, leverage, fee withdrawal, spread modelling, and margin architecture remain those frozen at commit `85847255bd2b3ec384db90f4b210ebc326e50499`. No optimisation, parameter selection, or strategy change is permitted for this run.

The isolated custom symbol is `GBP500_USDJPY_2019_2026`. It is imported from a linked replay beginning 2018.12.20 for indicator warm-up. The test interval begins 2019.01.01. The source datasets are assembled in precedence order from the 2019–2021, 2022, 2023, January-2025-gap, and current preserved datasets. The expected known generated-minute substitutions are 1,375 on 2021.10.01, 1,440 on 2022.03.01, and 586 on 2025.01.14: 3,401 minutes in total.

Acceptance reports numerical and execution-accounting checks, including the £500 opening balance, separately from the external GBP-conversion and capital-feasibility audits. Results are descriptive research evidence; they do not establish a live-trading outcome.
